COMPLETE DOCUMENT SUMMARY: Randolph Carter, 30, loses the key to his dream world, succumbing to reality's monotony. He tries to recapture his dreams through science, philosophy, and art, but fails. At 50, he discovers a silver key in an ancient box, which may unlock the gate to his lost dream world. Guided by his grandfather's spirit, Carter embarks on a journey to his ancestral home, seeking to merge with the past and reclaim his dreams. With the key in hand, he walks up the hill to his family's old mansion, leaving behind the modern world, and is greeted by the familiar voice of Old Benijah Corey, his uncle's former hired man, now over 100 years old. Carter's journey is a quest to rediscover the magic of his childhood and the secrets of his family's past, symbolized by the silver key, which may unlock not only the gate to his dreams but also the mysteries of his own identity and the universe.
