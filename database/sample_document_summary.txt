Randolph Carter, 50, lost the key to his dream world, succumbing to reality's prosiness. He tried science, philosophy, and social norms, but found them empty and shallow. He attempted to recapture his dreams through writing, occultism, and seeking out eccentric scholars, but to no avail. After a visit from his grandfather's ghost, Carter discovered a mysterious, ancient box containing a huge, tarnished silver key with cryptic arabesques. As he cleaned the key, his dreams intensified, calling him back to his ancestral past. He embarked on a journey to his family's old homestead in Arkham, leaving his car behind, and walked up the hill, feeling an otherworldly presence. The key, a potential gateway to his lost dream world, remained a mystery, but Carter's quest had begun, driven by a longing for beauty, magic, and the secrets of his family's past.
